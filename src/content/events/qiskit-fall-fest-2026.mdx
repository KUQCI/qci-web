QCI's IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 brings teams into a multi-track quantum computing competition shaped around real application challenges. Each track is hosted by a sponsor and supported through organizer guidance, workshops, mentorship sessions, and shortlisting checkpoints during the hacking period.

The festival begins with a hybrid opening-day information session, runs through two weeks of team development and initial submissions, and closes with one to two in-person days where selected participants present, compete, or demonstrate their solutions in the final format for their track.
